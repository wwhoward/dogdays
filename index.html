What an Amazing Day!

Dog Days of Summer 2025 was filled with games, activities, and unforgettable moments for dogs and their humans. Together, we raised money for dog rescues throughout Colorado while celebrating dogs of every age, size, and ability. Thank you to everyone who came out and made this day special!

See photos from the event! Meet the rescues we supported!

Every Dog Had Their Day

From young pups to senior dogs, athletic competitors to treat enthusiasts, every dog found games designed just for them. It was heartwarming to see dogs of all abilities participate and have a blast with their families!
