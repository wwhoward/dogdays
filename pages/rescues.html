Rescues

Dog Days of Summer is proud to support the following Colorado dog rescues:

CLAWA Animal Rescue

Based in Thornton, CLAWA Animal Rescue is an all breed rescue dedicated to rescuing & caring for abandoned dogs, abused dogs, dogs from high kill shelters, owner surrenders by finding safe and happy furever homes.

Harley's Hope

Our purpose is to help older, low-income pet parents and their companion/service animals remain together when issues arise. Our goal is to prevent at-risk animalss from being surrendured or euthanized for treatable conditions and temporary challenges. We help with major veterinary costs, emergency foster care, food support and more. We don't do adoptions; we help pets lucky enough to have homes remain in those homes by providing a number of support services.
