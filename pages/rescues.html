Rescues

Inspire Animal Hospital is excited to host the annual Dog Days of Summer Event in partnership with The Good Dog Academy! This fun-filled community event brings together dog lovers from across Colorado to celebrate our furry friends while supporting local rescue organizations.

The event will feature vendors, games, activities, giveaways, and opportunities for attendees to meet adoptable dogs from incredible local rescues. We are proud to support the rescue community and would love the opportunity to showcase your organization at this year's event.

Rescue organizations interested in participating are encouraged to apply by July 1st, 2026.
